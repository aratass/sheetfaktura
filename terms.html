2. Service

The add-on turns the data in an invoice sheet of a spreadsheet into files in the XRechnung (XML) and ZUGFeRD (PDF) formats and first checks the data for completeness and form. The tool does the same with an Excel file (.xlsx) filled in after the SheetFaktura template. The files are made in the user's browser. The add-on and the tool do not send or archive invoices.

The add-on and the tool check the format, not the tax content. They give no tax or legal advice. The user remains responsible for the correctness of the data, the choice of VAT treatment, and the storage and sending of invoices.
